Product Roadmap

The Future of RentFlow

We are constantly evolving to make renting seamless and secure. Here is a sneak peek at the exciting features we are building next.

Planned

OTP Email Login

Say goodbye to passwords. Soon, you'll be able to access your account instantly and securely using a One-Time Password sent directly to your registered email address.

Planned

Dedicated Dashboards

Tailored experiences are coming. Whether you are browsing for items to rent or you are an owner managing your listings, enjoy a fully personalized dashboard built for your specific role.

Planned

Email Booking Receipts

Keep track of your rentals easily. Once you book an item, a comprehensive receipt with the item details, lender's name, rental dates, and total amount will be emailed to you.

Planned

In-Platform Chat

Seamless communication. A built-in messaging system will allow renters and lenders to connect directly, ask questions, and negotiate terms without ever leaving the platform.

Planned

Verified Users (KYC)

To enhance trust and prevent fraud, we are introducing a "Verified Users" badge. Users will upload a government ID mock, which an admin manually approves. In a real-world scenario, this will integrate with 3rd-party KYC APIs to ensure data privacy while guaranteeing user authenticity.
