Frequently Asked Questions

RentFlow holds the 10% security deposit in escrow during your rental period. It is fully refunded to your original payment method within 3-5 business days after the item is returned in its original condition.
If an item is damaged, the repair costs will first be deducted from your security deposit. If the cost exceeds the deposit, RentFlow Support will mediate between you and the lender to resolve the issue based on the item's depreciated value.
You can use our in-app chat feature to speak directly with the lender before and during your rental. If you upgrade to RentFlow Pro, you instantly unlock phone numbers for direct calling.
For renters, there are NO platform fees. You only pay the rental rate and the refundable security deposit. For lenders (owners), RentFlow charges a flat 2% commission on the total rental earnings upon successful completion.
