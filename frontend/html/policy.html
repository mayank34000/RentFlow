Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Comprehensive guidelines, rules, and data policies for RentFlow users.

1. Information We Collect

RentFlow collects essential personal information including your full name, email address, contact number, and geographical location (city/address). We only collect data strictly necessary to facilitate local peer-to-peer rentals and connect lenders with borrowers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to create user profiles, process bookings, display relative locations of listings, and provide customer support. RentFlow does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing or advertising agencies.

3. Payment Security & Escrow

All financial transactions are processed securely through certified gateways (like Razorpay) using 256-bit SSL encryption. RentFlow never stores your credit card or bank details. Security deposits are held in a secure digital escrow until the rental period successfully concludes.

4. Platform Fee & Monetization

To maintain our secure infrastructure, escrow services, and customer support, RentFlow charges a nominal platform fee (e.g., 2% per transaction). This fee acts as an insurance premium to guarantee the safety of high-value assets listed on our platform.

5. General Damaged Products Policy

Borrowers are strictly responsible for returning items in the exact condition they were received. If an item is returned damaged, RentFlow reserves the right to withhold part or all of the security deposit to compensate the lender for repair or replacement costs.

6. Category-Specific Damage Rules

  • Electronics: Full liability for screen cracks, water damage, or missing accessories.
  • Vehicles: Deductions for scratches, dents, or engine issues caused by misuse. Gross negligence results in full liability.
  • Fashion: Normal wear is acceptable. Permanent stains or tears will result in cleaning/tailoring deductions.
  • Real Estate: Property damage (broken windows, damaged furniture) is deducted from the rental deposit.

7. Zero Liability for Offline Transactions

RentFlow provides guarantees only for transactions executed through our platform. If users choose to bypass our system and transact offline directly via phone numbers, RentFlow takes absolutely zero liability. We cannot verify offline handovers, secure deposits, or offer legal support for off-platform deals.

8. Fee Evasion & Account Suspension

Any attempt by users to intentionally bypass the platform's payment gateway to avoid paying the platform fee (platform leakage) is a direct violation of our terms. Such actions will result in immediate and permanent account suspension.

9. Dispute Resolution Mechanism

In the event of a disagreement between a lender and a borrower regarding item condition or return times, RentFlow will act as an impartial mediator. We will review digital logs, platform communications, and before/after photos provided by both parties to reach a fair verdict.

10. Listing Verification & Moderation

RentFlow reserves the right to review, edit, or remove any product listing that is deemed fraudulent, illegal, or in violation of community guidelines. Misrepresenting an item's condition or specifications will lead to listing removal and potential penalties.

11. User Responsibilities

Users are expected to provide accurate information when registering and listing products. It is the strict responsibility of the lender to ensure that the products listed are legally owned, properly maintained, and safe for rental use. Borrowers are expected to use rented items legally and within their intended purpose.

12. Policy Modifications

RentFlow reserves the right to update or modify these policies at any time to reflect changes in our platform architecture or legal requirements. Users will be notified of significant changes via their registered email address.
